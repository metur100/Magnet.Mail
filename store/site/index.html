Magnet Mail

Pull. Push. Deliver. A physics puzzle game for Android and iPhone.

About the game

You never touch the parcel. Hold the blue ATTRACT button to pull it towards the blue magnet and tap the red REPEL button to push it away from the red one. Launch it over walls, slingshot it around trains and gears, and land it gently in the right mailbox.

  • 30 levels in 6 worlds – from quiet streets to a space station
  • 6 parcel types with their own physics: light, heavy, fragile, bouncy, magnetic
  • A new Daily Delivery every day
  • 12 parcel designs and 6 mailboxes to collect
  • Free, no ads, no in-app purchases, plays fully offline

Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to certidevelopment@gmail.com – please mention your device and the level number.

Stuck on a level? After a failed attempt, choose Retry with a hint: the game shows a working route with the exact presses.

Lost progress? Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it; there is no cloud backup because the game has no account.

Privacy

Magnet Mail collects no data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
