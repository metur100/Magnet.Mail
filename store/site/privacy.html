Privacy Policy

App: Magnet Mail (Android package / iOS bundle ID com.certidevelopment.magnetmail)
Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

Magnet Mail does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game works completely offline.

Who is responsible

Medin Turkes, Düsseldorf, Germany – certidevelopment@gmail.com

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress the game saves the following only on your device (in the app's local storage):

  • completed levels, stars, best scores and impulse counts
  • unlocked worlds, parcels and mailboxes and your selected designs
  • daily challenge records and simple play statistics (number of deliveries, play time)
  • settings such as sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and left-handed mode

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Network access

The game makes no network requests. All graphics, sounds and fonts are included in the app. The Android system permission for internet access is part of the app framework template and is not used by the game.

Device features

If vibration is enabled, the game uses your device's vibration motor for haptic feedback. No sensor data, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone are accessed.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social login are included. Google Play and the Apple App Store, through which you download the app, process data under their own privacy policies.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children either. It contains no ads, no purchases and no communication features.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase personal data and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete – your game data exists only on your device and is under your control. For questions write to certidevelopment@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
